MOOH Driver Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

This policy explains what personal information the MOOH Driver app (the "App") collects, why, and what choices you have. The App is provided by Kiwi Campus, Inc. ("we", "us"), which operates the Nickelytics and Robot.com advertising programs. The App is for drivers registered with one of those programs.

Who can use the App

You can create an account in the App with your email address or with Sign in with Apple. A new account cannot start work sessions until we approve it for a driver program. The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

Information we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Email address When you create an account or sign in To send you a one-time sign-in code and identify your account.

Motion activity (for example, whether the phone detects driving) Only while a work session is active To keep measuring miles accurately when the vehicle moves slowly, for example in traffic.
Work session records: start and end times, how a session ended, and miles measured When you start and end a session To record the miles driven for the program you take part in.

Location

The App asks for location access, including "Always" access, so it can keep measuring miles when your phone is locked or you are using another app during a session. The App collects location only during a work session you started, and stops when you end it. When no session is active, the App does not collect your location. You can change location access at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Without it, the App cannot measure miles.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking software, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not collect location outside an active work session.

How information is shared

We share personal information only with service providers that host and operate the App's services for us, under obligations to protect it, and when required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Storage and security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in credentials are stored in your iPhone's Keychain. Points measured while offline are kept on your phone until they are uploaded. Signing out stops location collection at once; session data not yet uploaded stays on the phone and is uploaded only for the same driver after signing in again. Deleting the App removes its session data from your phone.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as your driver account is active and as needed to operate the program, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the App: with no work session active, tap Delete account on the main screen. This deletes your sign-in account at once, signs you out on your phone and, if you used Sign in with Apple, revokes the App's access to your Apple ID. Driver program records, such as work sessions and measured miles, are kept only as long as needed to settle the program and meet legal obligations, then deleted or made anonymous. To ask for them to be deleted sooner, email data@kiwicampus.com.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to close your account, in the App or by emailing data@kiwicampus.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be communicated through the App or by email.
