MOOH Driver Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy explains what personal information the MOOH Driver app (the "App") collects, why, and what choices you have. The App is provided by Kiwi Campus, Inc. ("we", "us"), which operates the Nickelytics and Robot.com advertising programs. The App is for drivers registered with one of those programs.

Who can use the App

You can create an account in the App with your email address or with Sign in with Apple. A new account cannot start work sessions until we approve it for a driver program. The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

Location

The App asks for location access, including "Always" access, so it can keep measuring miles when your phone is locked or you are using another app during a session. The App collects location only during a work session you started, and stops when you end it. When no session is active, the App does not collect your location. You can change location access at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Without it, the App cannot measure miles.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking software, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not collect location outside an active work session.

How information is shared

We share personal information only with service providers that host and operate the App's services for us, under obligations to protect it, and when required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Photos

The App never opens your camera and does not ask for access to your photo library. When you add a vehicle or license photo, your iPhone's photo picker lets you choose one photo, and the App receives only that photo. It is sent at once and is not kept in the App. Photos larger than 15 MB are not sent.

Storage and security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in credentials are stored in your iPhone's Keychain. Points measured while offline are kept on your phone until they are uploaded. Signing out stops location collection at once. If your sign-in expires during a work session, the session keeps measuring until you end it, and the App asks you to sign in again; session data not yet uploaded stays on the phone and is uploaded only for the same driver after signing in again. Deleting the App removes its session data from your phone.

How long we keep information

Location points measured during work sessions are deleted 90 days after the program validates the miles for that day. Temporary copies used to deliver those points to our systems are deleted after 7 days. We keep other personal information for as long as your driver account is active and as needed to operate the program, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the App: tap your initials at the top of the main screen, then Settings, then Delete account, and confirm. This ends any active work session, deletes your sign-in account at once, signs you out on your phone and, if you used Sign in with Apple, revokes the App's access to your Apple ID.

Deleting your account also makes your driver record anonymous at once: your name, email address, phone number, home address, date of birth, driver's license details and payment details are removed, and the photo of your driver's license is deleted. Photos of the vehicle are part of that vehicle's record in the program and are kept after you delete your account; to ask for them to be deleted, email data@kiwicampus.com. Driver program records are kept without any personal information, linked only to an anonymous ID:

  • Location points from a day whose miles the program validated are deleted 90 days after that validation.
  • Location points from a day that was never validated are deleted 90 days after you delete your account.
  • Work sessions, daily summaries and total miles are kept with no personal information.

To ask for these records to be deleted sooner, email data@kiwicampus.com.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to close your account, in the App or by emailing data@kiwicampus.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be communicated through the App or by email.
