Rads Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Rads driver app (the "App") collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what choices you have. It covers the App for iPhone and for Android. On your phone the App is called Rads. It is listed as MOOH Driver on the App Store and as Nickelytics on Google Play.

The App is provided by Kiwi Campus, Inc. ("we", "us"), which operates the Nickelytics and Robot.com advertising programs. The App is for drivers registered with one of those programs. It measures the miles your vehicle drives during a shift you start.

Who can use the App

You sign in with your email address and a one-time code we send to it. On iPhone you can also use Sign in with Apple. A new account cannot start shifts until we approve it for a driver program. The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

Location

The App asks for precise location and for location access at all times ("Always" on iPhone, "Allow all the time" on Android), so it can keep measuring miles when your phone is locked or you are using another app during a shift. The App collects location only during a shift you started, and stops when the shift ends. When no shift is active, the App does not collect your location.

On Android, a notification shows for the whole time a shift is recording. If your phone restarts during a shift, the shift you started continues after the restart.

You can change location access at any time in your phone's settings. Without it, the App cannot measure miles.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or third-party tracking software, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not collect location outside an active shift.

How information is shared

  • Service providers that host and operate the App's services for us receive personal information only to do that work, under obligations to protect it.
  • Advertisers whose campaign runs on your vehicle receive mileage totals. They do not receive your location.
  • We may also share information when required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Notifications

The App sends notifications only about a running shift: for example, to ask whether you are still driving when the vehicle has not moved for a long time, or to tell you that recording stopped, that your battery is low or that the phone has been offline. If you do not answer the "still driving" question, the App ends the shift itself. Notifications contain no location or other personal information. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings; the App keeps recording, and shows these messages only while it is open.

Photos

The App never opens your camera and does not ask for access to your photo library. When you add a vehicle or license photo, your phone's photo picker lets you choose one photo, and the App receives only that photo. It is sent at once and is not kept in the App. Photos larger than 15 MB are not sent.

Storage and security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in credentials are stored in your iPhone's Keychain, or on Android encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. Location points measured while offline are kept on your phone until they are uploaded. On Android they are kept in an encrypted database, and the App's data is left out of phone backups.

Signing out stops location collection. If your sign-in expires during a shift, the shift keeps measuring until it ends, and the App asks you to sign in again; data not yet uploaded stays on the phone and is uploaded only for the same driver after signing in again. Uninstalling the App removes its data from your phone.

How long we keep information

  • Individual location points from a shift are deleted 90 days after the program validates the miles for that day. Points from a day that has not been validated are kept until it is.
  • Temporary copies used to deliver location points within our systems are deleted 7 days after delivery.
  • For each day you drive, we keep a daily mileage record: the vehicle, the total miles and a simplified route of the day (a reduced list of map positions). Daily mileage records, shift records and total miles have no set deletion date and are kept after you delete your account.
  • We keep other personal information for as long as your driver account is active and as needed to operate the program, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the App:

  • iPhone: tap your initials at the top of the main screen, then Settings, then Delete account, and confirm.
  • Android: tap your initials at the top of the main screen, then Delete account, and confirm.

This ends any active shift, deletes your driver details and your sign-in account, and signs you out on your phone. On iPhone, if you used Sign in with Apple, it also revokes the App's access to your Apple ID. Without the App, email data@kiwicampus.com from the address you sign in with and we will delete your account (see Support).

Deleting your account makes your driver record anonymous at once: your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, driver's license details and payment details are removed, and the photo of your driver's license is deleted. Photos of the vehicle are part of that vehicle's record in the program and are kept after you delete your account; to ask for them to be deleted, email data@kiwicampus.com. Driver program records are kept without your name or contact details, linked only to an anonymous ID:

  • Location points from a day whose miles the program validated are deleted 90 days after that validation.
  • Location points from a day that was never validated are deleted 90 days after you delete your account.
  • Shift records, daily mileage records (including each day's simplified route) and total miles are kept.

To ask for these records to be deleted sooner, email data@kiwicampus.com.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to close your account, in the App or by emailing data@kiwicampus.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be communicated through the App or by email.
