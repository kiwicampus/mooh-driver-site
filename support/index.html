MOOH Driver Support

Help for drivers using the MOOH Driver app.

Contact us

Email data@kiwicampus.com or use the Robot.com contact form. Include the email address you sign in with.

Frequently asked questions

I did not receive my sign-in code.

Check your spam folder and make sure you entered the email address registered with your driver program. Codes expire, so request a new one if it has been a while. If you are not registered, contact us.

My miles are not counting.

Make sure a work session is active and that location access is set to "Always" in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > MOOH Driver, with Precise Location on. Motion & Fitness access also helps in slow traffic.

Does the app track me when I am not working?

No. Location is collected only while a work session you started is active. See the Privacy Policy.

I used the old Nickelytics app.

MOOH Driver replaces it. Install MOOH Driver and sign in with the email registered with your program.

How do I delete my account?

Email data@kiwicampus.com from your registered address and we will delete your account and personal information.
