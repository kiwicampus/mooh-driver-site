Rads Support

Help for drivers using the Rads app on iPhone or Android.

Contact us

Email data@kiwicampus.com or use the Robot.com contact form. Include the email address you sign in with.

Frequently asked questions

I did not receive my sign-in code.

Check your spam folder and make sure the email address is spelled correctly. Codes expire after 10 minutes, so request a new one if it has been a while. On iPhone you can also use Sign in with Apple.

My miles are not counting.

Make sure a shift is active and that the App has precise location at all times:

  • iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Rads: choose "Always" and turn Precise Location on. Motion & Fitness access also helps in slow traffic.
  • Android: Settings > Apps > Rads > Permissions > Location: choose "Allow all the time" and turn on "Use precise location". Physical activity access also helps in slow traffic.

I created an account, but I cannot start a shift.

New accounts need approval before they can start shifts. Once we approve your account and you have a vehicle, open the App and check again. If it is taking long, contact us with the email address you signed up with.

Does the app track me when I am not working?

No. Location is collected only while a shift you started is active. See the Privacy Policy.

I used the old Nickelytics app.

Rads replaces it. On Android, Rads arrives as an update of the Nickelytics app on Google Play. On iPhone, install Rads (listed as MOOH Driver on the App Store). Then sign in with the email address registered with your program.

How do I delete my account?

  • iPhone: tap your initials at the top of the main screen, then Settings, then Delete account, and confirm.
  • Android: tap your initials at the top of the main screen, then Delete account, and confirm.

You can also email data@kiwicampus.com from the address you sign in with, without using the App, and we will delete your account and personal information. The Privacy Policy explains what is deleted and what is kept.
