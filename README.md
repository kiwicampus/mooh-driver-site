# Rads driver site

Privacy policy and support pages for the Rads driver app, for iPhone (listed as MOOH Driver on the App Store) and Android (listed as Nickelytics on Google Play), served with GitHub Pages from `main`.

- `privacy/` - privacy policy (App Store Connect privacy policy URL, Google Play privacy policy URL)
- `support/` - support page (App Store Connect support URL, Google Play account deletion URL)

Plain HTML, no build step. Update the effective date in `privacy/index.html` whenever the policy changes. The policy must match the App Store privacy labels and the Google Play Data safety form.
